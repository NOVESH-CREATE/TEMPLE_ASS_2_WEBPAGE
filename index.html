TOP 10 TEMPLES IN INDIA

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

Located at : Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this temple stands on the western bank of the holy river Ganga. It is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas. The temple has been destroyed and rebuilt multiple times throughout history; the current structure was constructed by the Maratha monarch, Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore, in 1780.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (Varanasi), 25 km away.
  • By Rail: Varanasi Junction (BSB) is well-connected nationwide.
  • By Road: Taxis and auto-rickshaws are easily available across Varanasi city.

Tirupati Balaji Temple

Located at : Tirumala, Andhra Pradesh

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Also known as Venkateswara Temple, it is dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, an incarnation of Lord Vishnu. Constructed starting from 300 CE, it received major contributions from the Pallava, Chola, and Vijayanagara empires. It is famously one of the richest and most visited religious sites in the world .

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Tirupati Airport (Renigunta), 40 km from the hills.
  • By Rail: Tirupati Main Railway Station is the nearest railhead.
  • By Road:APSRTC operates frequent bus services up the Tirumala hills from Tirupati base camp.

Meenakshi Amman Temple

Located at : Meenakshi Amman Temple

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

A masterpiece of Dravidian architecture, this temple is dedicated to Meenakshi (a form of Parvati) and Sundareswarar (Shiva). Mentioned in texts as early as the 6th century, the current layout was largely rebuilt during the 16th and 17th centuries by the Nayak rulers, featuring 14 majestic gopurams (gateway towers).

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Madurai International Airport is 12 km from the temple.
  • By Rail:Madurai Junction is centrally located and highly accessible.
  • By Road:Local buses, auto-rickshaws, and app-based cabs operate continuously within the city.

Jagannath Temple

Located at : Puri, Odisha

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Built in the 12th century by King Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty, this temple is dedicated to Lord Jagannath (a form of Vishnu). It is a vital part of the holy Char Dham pilgrimage and is world-famous for its annual Ratha Yatra (Chariot Festival).

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Biju Patnaik International Airport (Bhubaneswar), roughly 60 km away.
  • By Rail: Puri Railway Station terminates several direct express trains.
  • By Road:Regulated bus services and cabs run via the scenic Puri-Bhubaneswar highway.

Somnath Temple

Located at : Veraval, Gujarat

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Somnath is traditionally considered the first among the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Shiva. Rising from the shores of the Arabian Sea, the temple has a tumultuous history of destruction by foreign invaders and resilient reconstructions, culminating in its final modern rebuild completed in 1951 led by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Diu Airport (85 km) or Rajkot Airport (200 km).
  • By Rail:Veraval Railway Station is just 5 km away from the shrine.
  • By Road:Well connected by Gujarat State Road Transport (GSRTC) buses from Ahmedabad.

Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib)

Located at : Amritsar, Punjab

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

The holiest gurdwara of Sikhism, its foundation stone was laid in 1589 by a Muslim Sufi saint, Mian Mir, emphasizing universal brotherhood. In 1830, Maharaja Ranjit Singh covered the sanctum with gold foil, giving it its iconic name. It stands as a supreme symbol of human equality.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport, 13 km from the city center.
  • By Rail: Amritsar Junction links directly to all major Indian metropolitan cities.
  • By Road:Free shuttle buses run frequently from the railway station to the temple complex.

Brihadeeswarar Temple

Located at : Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Built by Chola Emperor Raja Raja I between 1003 and 1010 CE, this temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Made entirely of granite, its massive tower (Vimana) rises 216 feet high and is capped by a single stone block weighing around 80 tons, representing a pinnacle of ancient engineering genius.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ), located 60 km away.
  • By Rail:Thanjavur Junction is well connected with Chennai and Trichy.
  • By Road:Local buses and private taxis are easily accessible from nearby cities.

Sun Temple

Located at : Konark, Odisha

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Built around 1250 CE by King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty, this monument is designed as a colossal chariot for the Sun God, Surya. It features 24 intricately carved stone wheels drawn by seven stone horses. It stands as an architectural triumph of ancient Kalinga stonecraft.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air: Biju Patnaik International Airport in Bhubaneswar is 65 km away.
  • By Rail: Puri Railway Station (35 km) or Bhubaneswar Station (60 km).
  • By Road: Excellent public buses and private day-taxis connect Konark directly to Puri.

Badrinath Temple

Located at : Badrinath, Uttarakhand

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Nestled in the Garhwal hill tracks along the Alaknanda River, this sacred shrine is dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Established as a premier pilgrimage center by Adi Shankara in the 8th century, it forms the core of both the national Char Dham and the Uttarakhand Chota Char Dham circuits.

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun), roughly 310 km away.
  • By Rail:Rishikesh (295 km) or Yog Nagari Rishikesh railway stations.
  • By Road: Accessible via National Highway 7 using buses or shared jeeps, open primarily from May to November.

Vaishno Devi Temple

Located at :Katra, Jammu & Kashmir

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Located in the Trikuta Mountains, this highly revered cave temple is dedicated to Vaishno Devi, a manifestation of the Hindu Mother Goddess. Millions of devotees undertake the holy uphill trek every year to seek blessings from the natural rock formations (Pindis).

How to Reach Info

  • By Air:ammu Airport (Satwari) is about 50 km from the base camp at Katra.
  • By Rail:Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SVDK) railway station runs direct trains.
  • By Road:From Katra, pilgrims complete the remaining 13 km trek on foot, by pony, or via battery cars/helicopters.
